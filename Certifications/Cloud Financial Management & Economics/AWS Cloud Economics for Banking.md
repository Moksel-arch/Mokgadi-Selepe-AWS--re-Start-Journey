I completed AWS Cloud Economics for Banking (January 2026).

This course showed how AWS brings economic benefits specially for banks.

I learned:

- Overview of AWS resources that fit banking needs (security, compliance, scalability)
- How cloud agility helps banks enter new markets faster
- Budget-saving tools and cost-control options
- Benefits of analytics, AI, and ML services for banking
- Real case studies of banks adopting AWS successfully

Really eye-opening – it proves cloud isn't just tech, it's a smart business move for finance too!
