I just completed **AWS SimuLearn: Get Started with Generative AI** (December 19, 2025). 

This immersive, simulation-based course was an exciting hands-on introduction to building with generative AI on AWS—leveraging my existing ML experience to quickly get practical with foundation models and real-world applications.

I learned:

- How to discover, test, and deploy large language models (LLMs) using **Amazon SageMaker JumpStart** for quick prototyping and production-ready inference
- Deploying models programmatically with the **Amazon SageMaker Python SDK** in Jupyter notebooks, including endpoint configuration
- Mastering **prompt engineering** techniques—like chain-of-thought, few-shot prompting, and iteration—to optimize model outputs for accuracy, relevance, and creativity
- Exploring generative AI workflows with services like Amazon Bedrock and SageMaker, tying into broader AWS AI tools

The interactive scenarios made applying these skills feel immediate and impactful. Perfect bridge from ML fundamentals to cutting-edge generative AI on AWS—super engaging and empowering!
