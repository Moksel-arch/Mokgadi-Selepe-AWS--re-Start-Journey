# **Cloud Financial Management & Economics**

This folder contains all my certificates from the Cloud Financial Management and Economics trainings on AWS.

These are the newest ones I completed in January 2026.

I did them because understanding costs and money in the cloud is very important for real jobs – no one wants to waste money on AWS!

I wanted to learn how to control spending, make smart budgets, and show the business value of cloud.
What I Learned in This Section

- Cloud Financial Management: FinOps Fundamentals & Strategies (Part 1)
The basic FinOps framework, AWS pricing model, cost optimization principles, and how to plan and report spending.
- Cloud Financial Management: FinOps Fundamentals & Strategies (Part 2)
Deeper FinOps practices – assigning costs to teams, rightsizing, reducing waste, better pricing options, and tracking sustainability impact.
- AWS Cloud Economics for Banking
How AWS saves money and brings business benefits specially for banks – agility, security, AI/ML use cases, and real banking success stories.
- Getting Started with Cloud Acquisition
How buying cloud services is different from traditional IT, what to check (pricing, security, data location, green impact), working with partners, and talking to legal/finance teams.

This section helps me become better at both building things in AWS and making sure they don’t cost too much.

It’s a perfect mix of tech and business skills!

I’m proud of finishing these four certificates quickly – now I feel more ready for cloud roles that care about cost control and value.

Thank you for looking at this part of my journey!
