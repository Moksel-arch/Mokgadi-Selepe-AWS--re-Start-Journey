**What the badge is about:**  

I completed the AWS Cloud Quest: Generative AI Practitioner training in December 2025. Here’s what that means:

- What I did: Finished a fun, gamified learning experience with hands-on labs to build real generative AI solutions on AWS.  
- What I earned: A “Trained” digital badge that shows I have practical skills in using AWS generative AI tools.  

Key things I learned (bullet points because that’s easier to read): 

- Basics of generative AI, including foundation models and how they work.  
- Amazon Bedrock – How to use it to access and build with models like Amazon Titan, Amazon Nova, Meta’s Llama, Mistral AI, and others.  
- Prompt engineering – Writing effective prompts to get the best results from AI models.  
- Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) – Improving AI responses by connecting models to external data.  
- Building practical solutions, such as:  
    - Generating code with AI tools.  
    - Creating chatbots.  
    - Adding content moderation to keep things safe.  
    - Fine-tuning models for better performance.  
- Using other AWS services like Amazon Q Developer and Amazon SageMaker in generative AI projects.  
- Responsible AI practices – Security, fairness, and guidelines for safe use.  
- Hands-on labs – Real-world scenarios in an interactive game (really engaging and fun!).  

All of this means I can now explain generative AI concepts, use AWS tools to build simple AI applications, and apply best practices for responsible and effective solutions. I’m now preparing for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam to take my skills further.
